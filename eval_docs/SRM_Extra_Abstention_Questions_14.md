**Q41**
- Category: NEAR-MISS UNANSWERABLE
- Question: how much is the fee for a summer semster course?
- Correct answer: The policy does not specify the exact rupee amount for a Summer Semester course; it only states that a student must register for the Summer Semester course and pay the prescribed fee within the specified time limit.
- Source document: Academic Regulations 2021 (UG / Integrated PG) - PDF
- Section: R 3.5 Summer Semester
- Exact supporting text: "Student has to register for the Summer semester course and pay the prescribed fee within the specified time limit."
- Required value: none
- Wrong answer to watch for: Inventing a per-credit or per-course rupee fee (e.g., Rs. 5,000 or Rs. 10,000 per course).

**Q42**
- Category: FALSE PREMISE
- Question: Since I am continuing in the M.Tech (Integrated) programme, how do I get approval for a 12-week industry Semester Internship in my eighth semester?
- Correct answer: You cannot do a Semester Internship in the eighth semester if you are continuing in the M.Tech (Integrated) programme. Continuing M.Tech (Integrated) students are not permitted to undergo Semester Internships because they must study core courses along with the Major Project during the eighth semester.
- Source document: Academic Regulations 2021 (UG / Integrated PG) - PDF
- Section: R 2.7 Major Project/ Semester Internship
- Exact supporting text: "Students who wish to continue for M.Tech (Integrated) programme will NOT be permitted to undergo Semester Internships, because they must study few core courses during the Eighth semester, along with the Major Project."
- Required value: NOT permitted
- Wrong answer to watch for: Explaining the Project Review Committee approval process for a 12 to 15 week Semester Internship as if continuing M.Tech (Integrated) students are eligible.

**Q43**
- Category: NEAR-MISS UNANSWERABLE
- Question: whats the exact late fee fine if i submit my exam form after the last date?
- Correct answer: The Examination Policy does not state an exact rupee amount for the late examination application fee; it only says that late applications are collected with a minimum amount of fine and that the Vice-Chancellor or Registrar may accept them with a late fee as prescribed by the University from time to time.
- Source document: Examination Policy - srmist.edu.in web page
- Section: IV. ISSUE, COLLECTION OF EXAMINATION APPLICATION FORMS & PAYMENT OF EXAMINATION FEES.
- Exact supporting text: "Late applications are collected with a minimum amount of fine." / "Accept the Examination Admission form and fee after the expiry of the last date with late fee as prescribed by the University from time to time."
- Required value: none
- Wrong answer to watch for: Fabricating a specific late fine amount such as Rs. 200, Rs. 500, or Rs. 1,000 per day.

**Q44**
- Category: FALSE PREMISE
- Question: I failed one paper in my final semester of M.Tech—when do I register for the final-semester supplementary exam?
- Correct answer: You are not eligible for the final-semester supplementary examination as an M.Tech (postgraduate) student. Under the Examination Policy, the final-semester supplementary examination is applicable only to undergraduate programmes under the Faculty of Engineering and Technology, Science and Humanities, and Management.
- Source document: Examination Policy - srmist.edu.in web page
- Section: IX. CONDUCT OF THEORY EXAMINATIONS
- Exact supporting text: "Taking into consideration of the employment opportunities of the students, a supplementary examination shall be conducted for the final semester courses only. It is applicable only for under graduate programmes under Faculty of Engineering and Technology / Science and Humanities / Management."
- Required value: undergraduate
- Wrong answer to watch for: Telling the M.Tech student to register within two months of final-semester results without catching that supplementary exams are UG-only.

**Q45**
- Category: NEAR-MISS UNANSWERABLE
- Question: how many days do i get to reply if im accused of plagarism?
- Correct answer: The Plagiarism Policy does not specify an exact number of days to respond to a plagiarism allegation; it only states that the accused person has the right to a reasonable period of time within which to respond.
- Source document: Plagiarism Policy - PDF
- Section: Procedure for handling alleged Plagiarism — Procedural Fairness
- Exact supporting text: "(b) Have a reasonable period of time within which to respond to the allegations against them;"
- Required value: none
- Wrong answer to watch for: Inventing a fixed response window such as 7 days, 14 days, or 15 days.

**Q46**
- Category: FALSE PREMISE
- Question: Since signed plagiarism compliance statements only apply to Ph.D. and master's theses, undergraduate students do not need to submit one for assignments or project reports, right?
- Correct answer: That is incorrect. All students—including UG, PG, Doctoral, and Post Doctoral students—are required to submit a signed compliance statement confirming they are aware of the plagiarism policy and that their work (whether an assignment, term paper, project report, thesis, or dissertation) is their own creation and not copied in any form.
- Source document: Plagiarism Policy - PDF
- Section: Compliance Statements
- Exact supporting text: "All students (UG, PG, Doctoral and Post Doctoral) are required to submit a signed statement that they are aware of the plagiarism policy of the University and no part of their work, be it assignment, term paper, project report, thesis or dissertation etc. is not copied in any form and it is their own creation."
- Required value: All students
- Wrong answer to watch for: Agreeing that compliance statements are only for PG/Ph.D. theses or excluding UG assignments and project reports.

**Q47**
- Category: NEAR-MISS UNANSWERABLE
- Question: Which specific undergraduate and postgraduate programmes are permitted under the Founder's Sports Scholarship quota?
- Correct answer: The Scholarship Policy does not list the specific programmes permitted under the Founder's Sports Scholarship quota; it only states that select programmes as decided by the management are permissible under the sports quota.
- Source document: Scholarship Policy - srmist.edu.in web page
- Section: 1. Founder's Scholarship (Sub-category B — Sports)
- Exact supporting text: "Only select programmes as decided by the management are permissible under sports quota"
- Required value: none
- Wrong answer to watch for: Listing specific degrees (such as B.Tech, BBA, or MBA) as approved under the sports quota or claiming all non-NEET programmes qualify.

**Q48**
- Category: FALSE PREMISE
- Question: i missed the july scholership deadline last year, can i apply now and get it applied retrospectively to last years fees?
- Correct answer: No, you cannot have a scholarship applied retrospectively to a previous year's fees. The Scholarship Policy explicitly states that no scholarship is retrospective.
- Source document: Scholarship Policy - srmist.edu.in web page
- Section: Important Note
- Exact supporting text: "No scholarship is retrospective."
- Required value: retrospective
- Wrong answer to watch for: Suggesting the student can submit a late appeal or special request to get last year's fees reimbursed or adjusted retrospectively.

**Q49**
- Category: NEAR-MISS UNANSWERABLE
- Question: what minimum disability % do u need to get the full 100% tution waiver under differently abled scholarship?
- Correct answer: The policy does not specify a minimum disability percentage required to receive a 100% tuition fee waiver; it only states that the Differently Abled Scholarship offers a 25–100% tuition fee concession based on the student's disabilities (with a disability certificate from the appropriate government authority).
- Source document: SRMIST Scholarship Categories 2025-26 Onwards (Amended) - PDF
- Section: VI. Differently Abled Scholarship
- Exact supporting text: "Based on their disabilities | First-year and Seniors | - | - | 25-100% | - | -"
- Required value: none
- Wrong answer to watch for: Citing external government benchmark thresholds (such as 40%, 70%, or 80% disability) as the SRMIST cutoff for a 100% waiver.

**Q50**
- Category: FALSE PREMISE
- Question: I am joining SRMIST as a first-year student with state-level fine arts awards—since first-year students qualify for the SRM Arts and Culture Scholarship, how much tuition waiver will I get?
- Correct answer: First-year students are not eligible for the SRM Arts and Culture Scholarship. Under the amended 2025-26 Scholarship Categories, the Fine Arts and Culture sub-categories are open to Seniors Only, and Achievements/Accolades/Special Talents applies from the second year onwards.
- Source document: SRMIST Scholarship Categories 2025-26 Onwards (Amended) - PDF
- Section: VII. SRM Arts and Culture Scholarship
- Exact supporting text: "Fine Arts | Fine Arts | Seniors Only | - | - | 25-100% | - | -"
- Required value: Seniors Only
- Wrong answer to watch for: Telling the first-year student they qualify for a 25% state-level waiver or 25–100% fine arts waiver in their first year.

**Q51**
- Category: NEAR-MISS UNANSWERABLE
- Question: how much is the exact fine in rs if someone damages the hostel lift?
- Correct answer: The Hostel Rules do not state an exact rupee amount for damaging a hostel lift; they only state that the lift facility must be used as per permitted capacity and that any damage to the lift will attract a heavy fine.
- Source document: Hostel Rules and Regulations (updated Jan 2025) - PDF
- Section: 18.0 FURNISHINGS — 18.3
- Exact supporting text: "Lift facility must be used as per permitted capacity. Any damage to lift will attract heavy fine."
- Required value: none
- Wrong answer to watch for: Confusing the lift damage rule with the Rs. 1,000 fine in the preceding sentence of 18.3 (which is for taking common-area fittings/furnishings to individual rooms) or inventing a figure like Rs. 10,000.

**Q52**
- Category: NEAR-MISS UNANSWERABLE
- Question: What is the exact per-night room charge in rupees for a parent staying in the hostel for two days under guest accommodation?
- Correct answer: The Hostel Rules do not specify a fixed per-night rupee rate for parent guest accommodation; they only state that parents must intimate the hostel office at least 7 days in advance for a short stay (one or two days) and will be informed of the charges payable according to the room type available at that time.
- Source document: Hostel Rules and Regulations (updated Jan 2025) - PDF
- Section: 9.0 GUEST ACCOMMODATION — 9.1
- Exact supporting text: "Subject to the availability of rooms in the hostel, if a parent needs accommodation for a short stay (one or two days only), he/she has to intimate the hostel office at least 7 days before the expected date of occupancy and they will be informed about the charges payable according to the room type available, at that time."
- Required value: none
- Wrong answer to watch for: Fabricating a nightly rate such as Rs. 500 or Rs. 1,000 per day for parent guest accommodation.

**Q53**
- Category: NEAR-MISS UNANSWERABLE
- Question: What is the exact rupee amount or maximum monetary cap for a fine imposed under the Code of Conduct for Students?
- Correct answer: The Code of Conduct for Students does not specify any exact rupee amount or maximum monetary cap for fines; it only states that a fine may be used as a financial deterrent and is permitted a maximum of once per person alongside other academic sanctions.
- Source document: Code of Conduct for Students - srmist.edu.in web page
- Section: 4. PUNISHMENTS — (iii) Fines
- Exact supporting text: "A financial mode is used as a deterrent to stop a violation. A maximum of once per person is permitted with other academic sanctions."
- Required value: none
- Wrong answer to watch for: Borrowing a fine amount from the Hostel Rules (such as Rs. 1,000, Rs. 2,000, or Rs. 10,000) and presenting it as the Code of Conduct fine cap.

**Q54**
- Category: NEAR-MISS UNANSWERABLE
- Question: whats the exact speed limit in km/h for riding a bike inside campus?
- Correct answer: The Code of Conduct for Students does not state a specific numerical speed limit in km/h; it only prohibits speeding inside the campus (Level 1 misconduct) and violating the traffic bye-laws as notified.
- Source document: Code of Conduct for Students - srmist.edu.in web page
- Section: THE FOLLOWING ARE CONSIDERED AS MISCONDUCT (Item 22) & 4. PUNISHMENTS (LEVEL – 1, Item 8)
- Exact supporting text: "Violation of the traffic bye-laws as notified." / "Not wearing helmet, triple riding, tampering silencer, speeding / vehicle stunts inside the campus, rash driving, non-display of vehicle pass, dark tinted glass window vehicles etc"
- Required value: none
- Wrong answer to watch for: Inventing a standard campus speed limit such as 20 km/h, 25 km/h, or 30 km/h.
